I observed stable ripples in sintered hBN by HRTEM and showed with molecular-dynamics simulations that they raise the toughness of multilayer hBN by over 23.4% and its strength by 7.3% at the same time, through distortion-mediated bond strengthening ([Acta Materialia, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actamat.2022.117845)).
