## Digital Twin Engineer (optimisation and AI engineering) · Gradyent · 12/2025 – present

Gradyent's digital-twin platform recommends the optimal operation of industrial and district-heating networks. I work on its optimisation software and build AI tooling.

* **Optimisation:** I helped deliver over 3x faster compute and ~97% lower peak memory for a live optimisation service, and designed and built configurable, backward-compatible features for different client networks.
* **AI engineering:** I designed and built a multi-agent development harness with trace-based evaluation and guardrail hooks, which most of the development team now uses as its standard way of working. To get there, I led team-wide discussions on a shared development process and ran workshops on it.

## Early career fellow (modelling and simulations) · IIT Gandhinagar, India · 09/2022 – 11/2023

A competitively awarded fellowship for my own, independent research project.

* Built a Python package, on top of an open-source graphene toolkit, that generates polycrystalline graphene and hBN structures with graded grain sizes, and used it in molecular-dynamics heat-transport and phonon simulations of graded polycrystalline graphene (up to ~1.04M atoms), writing the Python analysis that identified the competing mechanisms behind its thermal rectification (first author, Carbon 2024).
* Designed and taught, as the sole instructor, a 10-day hands-on graduate course on deep learning for mechanical, molecular and materials engineering, with TensorFlow (Keras) labs in Python, for 22 graduate students from different fields.

## PhD candidate (modelling and simulations) · Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China · 09/2018 – 06/2022

* Calibrated an anisotropic (Tsai-Wu) failure criterion for multilayer hBN from over 35 molecular-dynamics failure points, with stress-space accuracy measures I derived: a mean percent offset of −1.43% (SD 5.5%) from the fitted surface, against 23.3% and 25.12% for the Hill and maximum-stress criteria (first author, Mechanics of Materials 2022, front cover).
* Observed stable ripples in sintered hBN by HRTEM and showed with molecular-dynamics simulations that they raise the toughness of multilayer hBN by over 23.4% and its strength by 7.3% at the same time, through distortion-mediated bond strengthening (first author, Acta Materialia 2022).
